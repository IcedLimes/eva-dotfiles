# Credits

## Wallpaper

`assets/wallpapers/eva01-mono-parallax-flipped.png` (the desktop) and
`eva01-mono-flipped.png` (the lock screen) are edits of a black-and-white
Unit-01 phone wallpaper by an artist I couldn't identify: upscaled, mirrored,
toned to pure black and white, and extended to 16:10 with a margin for the
parallax. The original carries a small vertical signature in its lower-right
corner (lower-left here, mirrored), too small to read at the sizes it
circulates in.

Where it was found:

- Pinterest: <https://i.pinimg.com/originals/fd/e3/a1/fde3a13f23389dae0d2bd2d765d569f5.jpg>
- Wallpaper Cave, *Evangelion AMOLED wallpapers*: <https://wallpapercave.com/w/wp14882778>

Reverse image searches (SauceNAO, Yandex, Bing, TinEye) turned up only
reposts. **If this is your work**, please open an issue: I'll credit you
properly, or take it out if you'd rather.

## Rei

`config/eva/rei-frames.json`: Rei in braille, traced frame by frame from the
*Fly Me to the Moon* ending credits of *Neon Genesis Evangelion*
(`config/eva/tools/fly2braille.py` makes them from the GIF).
